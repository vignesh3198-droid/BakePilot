BakePilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026 • Version 1.1.43

Welcome to BakePilot ("we", "our", or "us"), crafted by MOOVAR Kreativ. BakePilot is a business operating system designed specifically for independent home bakers, cloud kitchens, and artisan patisseries.

1. Data Ownership & 100% Confidentiality Guarantee

We respect the confidentiality of your culinary creations and customer relationships:

  • Your Recipes are 100% Confidential: Your recipes, ingredient formulas, cost margins, and secret ratios remain strictly private. We never share, sell, or inspect your proprietary formulas.
  • Your Client Database Belongs Exclusively to You: Your customer names, phone numbers, delivery addresses, and purchase histories are exclusively your property. BakePilot will never contact your clients or sell your lead list.

2. Information We Collect

To provide billing, invoicing, recipe costing, and data synchronization, BakePilot processes:

  • Account Details: Bakery name, business email, and encrypted password hash.
  • Operational Bakery Data: Product catalog, ingredient inventory, orders, customer contact info, expenses, and cashbook records created by you in the app.
  • Device & Diagnostics: App version, operating system, and anonymous performance logs to ensure 24/7 reliability.

3. How Information is Used

  • To generate GST & Non-GST bills and WhatsApp-shareable invoices.
  • To calculate accurate recipe costs, profit margins, and ingredient batch requirements.
  • To sync your bakery database securely between your mobile device and your private cloud database.
  • To compile your automated End-Of-Day (EOD) business summaries.

4. Data Storage and Security

Each registered bakery is assigned an isolated, sandboxed database. All transmissions between the mobile app and server utilize HTTPS encryption. Automated rolling backup snapshots are archived to prevent data loss.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade any personal or business data to third-party advertising networks. Invoices and bills shared via WhatsApp are sent directly through your device's native WhatsApp application without intermediate third-party interception.
